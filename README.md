# why-me

> A personal narrative experience about growing up feeling judged, unloved, and alone.

## What is this?

**why-me** is a narrative-driven project that tries to make the player *feel* what it is like to live as someone who:

- Rarely received complete affection since childhood
- Was frequently blamed and looked down upon
- Eventually chose silence and isolation because it felt safer than dealing with people who always think they are right
- Still carries the weight of those early experiences until now

The core idea is simple but heavy:

**Let the player experience life from this point of view**, and understand how deeply the way parents think can shape a person's entire life.

This is not a game about teaching morals in a preachy way.  
It is an attempt to make someone *feel* the quiet damage that judgment and lack of understanding can cause.

---

## Core Message

Humans were given the ability to think.  
Yet many still choose to judge, insult, and dismiss others without ever trying to understand the pain behind them.

This project exists because being on the receiving end of that treatment leaves marks that do not easily disappear.

---

## Vision

The player should not just watch a story.  
They should walk through it.

They should feel:
- The small moments that slowly build walls
- The weight of always being the one at fault
- The quiet decision to withdraw
- The complicated relationship with the way parents think and respond
- The strange safety found in being alone

---

## Direction

This project prioritizes **feeling and perspective** over graphics or complex systems.

Possible forms (still open):
- Strong first-person narrative (text-based or semi-visual)
- Simple low-poly 3D environments (if feasible)
- Choice-driven moments that reflect limited options and emotional consequences

The technical approach will stay as close as possible to full custom coding, avoiding large commercial engines when possible.

---

## Development Philosophy

- The owner provides the vision, details, and corrections
- AI assistants handle the implementation
- Progress is expected to be slow and steady
- Honesty over polish
- Feeling over spectacle

---

## Status

**Early concept stage.**

No playable version exists yet.  
This repository currently serves as the foundation for the idea, structure, and future development.

## Note from the creator

This is personal.

It comes from real experiences of feeling unseen, frequently blamed, and choosing isolation because dealing with judgmental people felt heavier than being alone.

If this project ever makes even one person pause and think twice before judging someone else, then it already has meaning.
